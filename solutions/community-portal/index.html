One home for the whole community

Shape the portal around your members, with configurable homepages, spaces for different groups, and clear publishing and access controls.

Configurable home
Build each portal home from configurable widgets that surface relevant updates, events, resources, activity, and shortcuts.
Feed and announcements
Publish official announcements alongside member posts, images, links, reactions, replies, and pinned content, with clear audience controls.
Groups and discussions
Give committees, chapters, projects, and interest groups public, private, or invite-only spaces with their own feeds, threaded discussions, and Q&A.
People and resources
Help members find people, expertise, documents, collections, events, and guidance through connected search and discovery.

A home that adapts to every role

Give every role a relevant starting point. Configurable widgets and role-aware navigation bring forward the updates, events, resources, and conversations that matter now.

Configurable widgets

Choose the updates, events, resources, activity, and shortcuts that belong on each portal home, then arrange them around the community's priorities.

Content for each role

Show the right destinations, content, and actions to members, group leaders, contributors, moderators, and administrators.

Timely highlights

Bring upcoming events, recent resources, important notices, and active conversations forward when they are most relevant.

Your brand, consistently

Carry your identity, terminology, structure, and priorities through a familiar experience on every device.

Conversation with structure

Combine the reach of a social feed with the focus of groups, threads, and Q&A. Members can share quickly, respond openly, and continue substantial topics without losing context.

Posts and announcements

Publish organisation updates and let members share text, images, links, and media with the audiences permitted for each space.

Reactions and replies

Turn updates into participation through reactions, comments, replies, and a visible conversation history.

Purpose-built group spaces

Support committees, chapters, projects, cohorts, and interests with membership controls, dedicated feeds, and focused participation.

Discussions and Q&A

Keep substantial topics easy to follow through threads, questions, answers, and searchable outcomes.

Find people, resources, and expertise

Turn scattered knowledge into a connected community resource. Members can find expertise, browse curated collections, and move from relevant content to the people, groups, discussions, and events around it.

Resource collections

Organise documents, links, guidance, media, and reference material into clear, curated collections.

Search and filtering

Help members narrow large libraries by topic, type, language, group, or attributes configured for the organisation.

Member directory and profiles

Make members and expertise discoverable through structured profiles, affiliations, interests, areas of expertise, and contact information.

Related people and resources

Connect relevant people and resources to the groups, discussions, announcements, and events where members need them.

Open participation, clear control

Let members participate openly where appropriate while roles, permissions, private spaces, moderation, and publishing controls keep each area under clear organisational control.

Roles and permissions

Control who can view, publish, reply, moderate, manage groups, and administer each area of the portal.

Private spaces

Keep committees, leadership groups, projects, and restricted resources accessible only to the right members.

Moderation controls

Support clear community standards with reporting, review, publishing controls, and accountable moderation.

Targeted publishing

Direct announcements and content to specific roles, groups, chapters, or audiences so members receive what is relevant without unnecessary noise.
